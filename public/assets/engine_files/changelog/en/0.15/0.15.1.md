### New Features

#### Engine Editor

- **Art Manager.** The character art popup now edits the whole look in one place, with the doll previewing every change live. Five tabs – Layers, Attributes, Spine, Face and Offset – tick skin layers on and off (each row shows the image or animation key it resolves to for the current attributes, red when nothing matches), pick attribute values from dropdowns, add or swap the per-view spine atlas and skeleton, set the static face image and its precedence beside the face crop, and tune the per-view art offset by dragging the doll or scrolling over it to scale. The pencil on a layer row saves and jumps to that layer in Skin Layers. Available wherever the button was: character templates, statuses, item templates and skill slots.
