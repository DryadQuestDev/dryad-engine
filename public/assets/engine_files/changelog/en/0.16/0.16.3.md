### New Features

#### Editor

- **Party line lint.** The Dungeon Content Editor warns when an `id!:` party line carries actions beyond staging – a menu, a flag, a jump, an item. They are skipped with the line while that character is away, and a `{…}` line written right under the text is part of it. Staging (`actor`, `asset`, `sound`, `music`, `animate`, `grade` and the like) is left alone.

### Bug Fixes

- **A skipped paragraph keeps its choices.** A party line (`id!:`) whose character is away, or a paragraph an `if{}` left empty, used to take its `>` choices with it – and when it closed its block, the `~` branches too, so the reader landed in the first branch or dropped out of the scene. The text is still skipped, but its choices now replace "continue" on the paragraph before it. When the reader arrives on such a paragraph by a jump instead, the text already on screen stays and the choices show under it.
