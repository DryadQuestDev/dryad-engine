### Bug Fixes

- **A background replaces the one before it.** Staging a `bg` asset used to leave every earlier background on stage beneath it, room defaults included, so a long scene piled up full-screen images. Weaker GPUs, phones in particular, flickered under the load and drew pictures late. Now the new background takes over: the old one stays until the new one has faded in, then leaves, so the change never dips to black. One drawn above the newcomer exits with its own animation.
- **No black frames between scenes.** A scene ending into another, an `{enter}` into a room or dungeon whose event opens a new scene, and `{asset: "false, forest"}` all emptied the stage at once, so the incoming background faded in over black. The background on screen now stays until the incoming one has faded in over it, and the same background carried into the next scene simply stays. A scene that ends with nothing after it clears the stage as before.
- **Entering another dungeon from a scene works again.** An `{enter: "dungeon.room"}` played from a scene could stop half-way when the room being left has no namesake in the new dungeon: the map switched, the player kept the old room, landed off the map and every move failed with "Cannot reach that location".
- **Map encounters near the compass are clickable again.** The current room's compass draws above every encounter, and its whole box caught clicks, so an encounter image or polygon near the room's center could not be selected. Only the compass arrows catch clicks now.
- **The battle result Continue button stays on screen (rpg_battler).** A tall result panel: rewards, level-ups, loot could push Continue below a short screen, such as a phone held sideways, with no way to scroll to it. The result card now fits the screen: the Victory or Defeat title and Continue stay in place and the content between them scrolls.
